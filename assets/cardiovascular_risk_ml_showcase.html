Fairness audit checklist

Evaluate whether model errors and performance differ across demographic groups.

◈
Group-wise performance
Compare ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, precision, recall and F1 where sample sizes allow.
◈
Error-rate differences
Inspect false-negative and false-positive rates across age, sex and other available groups.
◈
Calibration and limitations
Check predicted-risk calibration and report small-group uncertainty.
◈
Responsible interpretation
Treat this as research support, not a diagnosis or clinical decision tool.

Research workflow

A reproducible pipeline from raw data to model interpretation.
01 / DATA

Explore & clean

Review missing values, target definition, feature types and possible leakage.

02 / PREP

Split & balance

Use a stratified split. Apply SMOTE to training data only, inside the pipeline.

03 / TRAIN

Fit four models

Use consistent preprocessing and a validation strategy for fair comparison.

04 / EVALUATE

Compare metrics

Report ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, F1 and confusion-matrix results on held-out data.

05 / EXPLAIN

SHAP & audit

Explain model behaviour and check subgroup performance with uncertainty.
